Questionnaire: Brand Perception

Measures trust, professionalism, and perceived identity of the brand behind the design.

  • BP1Scale 1–5This design makes the brand seem trustworthy.
  • BP2Scale 1–5The design gives me confidence in the brand's professionalism.
  • BP5Scale 1–5I would consider buying a product from this brand.
  • BP6ChoiceWhich brand personality fits this design? (Sincere, Exciting, Competent, Sophisticated, Rugged)
  • BP8OpenDescribe the brand in one word, based on this design.
Report

The Zephyr Vision ad scores high on trustworthiness (M=4.31, SD=0.72) and professionalism (M=4.48, SD=0.61). The dominant brand personality is Sophisticated (42.1%) followed by Competent (28.7%). 81.3% perceive the brand as innovative.

Purchase intent is the weakest metric (M=3.28, SD=1.04) — respondents trust the brand but lack a clear next step. Open-ended responses frequently cite "sleek" and "futuristic", but several note "I don't know what to do next".
